# SalesPilot AI — Product Overview & Capabilities

SalesPilot AI is an autonomous B2B revenue and inbound sales assistant platform built for high-velocity SaaS and enterprise sales teams.

## Core Capabilities
- **Autonomous Voice & Text Engagement:** Answers inbound prospect questions conversationally, handles common product objections, and asks structured discovery questions.
- **Knowledge-Grounded Responses (RAG):** Retrieves verified product, pricing, and security facts directly from indexed company documentation before responding, preventing fabricated product claims.
- **Intelligent Lead Qualification:** Captures prospect name, company, headcount, requirements, budget, and timeline, scoring prospects into HOT, WARM, and COLD tiers using transparent prototype qualification rules.
- **Automated Appointment Scheduling:** Checks real-time calendar slot availability, prevents double booking, and records confirmed product demos linked directly to CRM lead records.
- **CRM & Call Summary Automation:** Automatically creates and updates lead profiles, logs multi-turn transcripts, and generates structured post-call executive summaries.

## Supported Channels
- Browser-based Web Voice Interface (Web Speech API compatible in Chrome and Edge).
- Web Chat / Text Console with instant synchronization to the CRM workspace.
- Dedicated Telephony/SIP & Enterprise STT/TTS adapters (available on Enterprise plans).
