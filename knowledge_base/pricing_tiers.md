# SalesPilot AI — Pricing Plans & Packaging

All SalesPilot AI subscriptions include access to the web CRM workspace, conversation transcript logging, and rule-based lead qualification.

## Plan Tiers

### 1. Starter Plan — $299 / month
- Up to 500 AI sales conversations per month.
- Up to 3 sales team seats.
- Standard Knowledge Base indexing (up to 25 documents).
- Automated lead capture and email/calendar demo booking.
- Email support with 24-hour SLA.

### 2. Growth Plan — $899 / month (Most Popular)
- Up to 2,500 AI sales conversations per month.
- Up to 15 sales team seats.
- Advanced Knowledge Base indexing (up to 250 documents).
- Real-time text sentiment and objection detection.
- Automated AI call summaries and CRM webhook sync.
- Priority chat and email support (4-hour SLA).

### 3. Enterprise Plan — Custom Pricing (Starting at $2,400 / month)
- Unlimited or high-volume custom conversation tiers (10,000+ monthly conversations).
- Unlimited team seats and role-based access control (RBAC).
- Dedicated vector index isolation and PostgreSQL deployment option.
- Custom CRM integrations (Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho) and dedicated telephony connectors.
- SOC 2 Type II compliance package, SSO/SAML, and dedicated Solutions Engineer.

## Billing & Trials
- Annual billing receives a 15% discount compared to monthly billing.
- A 14-day guided pilot is available for qualified Growth and Enterprise prospects.
- No setup fee for Starter or Growth; Enterprise onboarding includes white-glove knowledge base tuning.
