# SalesPilot AI — Integrations, Onboarding & FAQ

## CRM & Calendar Integrations
- **Built-in CRM:** Works out of the box with no external CRM required; stores leads, qualification scores, transcripts, and call summaries.
- **External CRM Sync (Growth & Enterprise):** REST APIs and webhook connectors allow exporting qualified leads and call summaries to HubSpot, Salesforce, and Pipedrive.
- **Scheduling:** Built-in double-booking-safe slot management during demo calls; Enterprise tiers also support Google Calendar and Microsoft 365 availability sync.

## Onboarding Timeline
- **Starter & Growth:** Self-serve setup takes under 30 minutes by uploading product PDFs/Markdown docs and configuring business hours.
- **Enterprise:** Typical rollout takes 2 to 4 weeks, including SSO setup, custom CRM field mapping, and sales playbook calibration.

## Frequently Asked Questions
- **Does SalesPilot AI replace human account executives?**
  No. SalesPilot AI handles repetitive inbound product Q&A, early discovery, lead scoring, and demo booking so human Account Executives can focus on closing qualified opportunities.
- **What happens if a prospect asks something not in the documents?**
  SalesPilot AI is instructed to admit when specific details are unavailable in the knowledge base and offer to connect the prospect with a human specialist during a scheduled demo.
- **Can we customize qualification rules?**
  Yes. Qualification weights for budget, timeline, urgency, and company fit can be adjusted in Python/configuration to match your sales team's Ideal Customer Profile (ICP).
